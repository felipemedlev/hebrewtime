# Task 09 — Curriculum & Narrator

## Narrator Persona

**Noa (נועה)** — a warm, friendly Israeli woman in her late 20s living in Tel Aviv. She speaks slowly and clearly in modern spoken Hebrew. She addresses listeners directly as if they're a friend learning Hebrew. Consistent first-person voice across all 20 episodes.

## TTS Voice

- Language: `he-IL`
- Gender: female
- Tone prompt: "Read aloud in a warm, welcoming, conversational tone. Speak slowly and clearly for Hebrew learners."

## Episode Progression (~20 × ~10 min)

Each episode now has three content layers:

- **Narrative hook:** a small everyday scene or problem from Noa's life, so the episode feels like a real podcast story.
- **High-frequency vocabulary:** useful beginner words and function words, not rare topic nouns.
- **Conversational chunks:** reusable phrases learners can actually say in daily life.

## Vocabulary Principles

- Prioritize core words learners need constantly: pronouns, common verbs, question words, prepositions, time words, and practical social phrases.
- Keep topic vocabulary small and useful. For example, a supermarket episode should teach `צריך`, `רוצה`, `כמה`, `עולה`, `שקית`, not long product lists.
- Reinforce words across contexts. `רוצה`, `צריך`, `יש`, `אין`, `איפה`, `מתי`, `אפשר`, and `בסדר` should appear in many episodes.
- Use natural spoken Hebrew chunks learners can memorize as units, such as `אני לא יודעת`, `זה בסדר`, `אפשר...?`, and `לאט לאט`.

## Story Rules

- Open each episode inside a concrete scene from Noa's day.
- Include one tiny problem, choice, surprise, or emotional moment.
- End with a warm reflection or direct encouragement to the listener.
- Avoid scripts that sound like vocabulary lists, grammar lectures, or textbook dialogues.
