# Make time for Hebrew.

Listen to a story. Understand the words. Build your vocabulary.

Join **HebrewTales**, a place to read, listen, and build your Hebrew vocabulary. Explore stories and useful words, with tools that help you keep practicing at your own pace.

Whether you are learning your first sentences or ready for more advanced material, choose a level and start with one episode.

## What you can do

### Listen and read together

Explore podcast episodes and lessons created with AI. Follow the Hebrew text as you listen, replay a section, and return to your saved listening position.

### Understand with translations

Read Hebrew alongside English, Russian, Spanish, or French. Show translations when you need support and hide them when you want to challenge yourself.

### Explore the words you meet

Click a Hebrew word to get help with its meaning in context. Explore dictionary details, pronunciation, and verb forms where available.

### Build your own vocabulary

Save useful words from your reading, or add your own words and phrases. Keep them together in a searchable vocabulary list, with example phrases to help you practice using them.

### Review in four ways

Use flashcards with spaced repetition, practice recalling the Hebrew from a translation, match words with their meanings, or complete missing words in sentences. Your saved vocabulary becomes your practice material.

### Find your level

Choose beginner, intermediate, or advanced material. Explore the original podcast track and additional learning tracks created with AI.

### See your progress

Mark lessons as finished, see which words are due for review, and follow your practice activity and accuracy. Resume an unfinished flashcard session when you return.
